Momentum rotation

Edge doesn't marry a coin. The engine scores the memecoin field on liquidity and momentum, and rotates the basket toward strength — mechanically, on schedule, never on vibes.

🎯

Drift-band rebalancing

The same engine that runs the core portfolios, tuned hot: tighter bands, 24/7 marks. When a runner blows past its weight, Edge trims into strength and recycles into laggards.

Hard risk rails

Equal-weight entries. A hard per-name cap. No leverage, ever. And a basket-level drawdown trigger that de-risks to USDC — aggression with a seatbelt, not a death wish.
