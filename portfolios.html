The core discipline

How every portfolio stays on target

You choose a target mix. Markets constantly push it out of shape — winners grow too large, laggards shrink. Kestrel watches the drift and, when a holding strays past its band, trims what ran ahead and tops up what fell behind — back to your target.

Drift-band, not calendar

Kestrel rebalances only when a holding drifts past its band — often in volatile stretches, rarely in calm ones. No needless trading.

Non-custodial by design

Your assets stay in your wallet. Kestrel can rebalance between your holdings but can never withdraw to itself.

Real, tokenized securities

Holdings are 1:1 tokenized US stocks, ETFs, gold and silver via Backpack Securities — tradeable 24/7 on Solana. The metals sleeve allocates across gold (GLD) and silver (SILV, by Dominion).
